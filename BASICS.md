## The basics
- **3 to 6 players** per game. A match is **3 rounds** by default (the host can set up to 6).
- Every round you build one Turkish word from a **root** plus **suffixes** — then everyone tries to spot whose word is real and whose is invented.
- Most points at the end wins. The game is named after the in-between thing you make: an **uydurum** — a word that isn't in the dictionary but sounds like it could be.

## Each round has three phases

**1. Pick a root (15 seconds)**
- A pool of real dictionary roots is on screen. Everyone secretly ranks their 3 favorites — nobody sees anyone else's choices.
- When time's up, picks are revealed. If two players wanted the same root, it goes to **whoever has the lowest score** (a built-in comeback mechanic); if still tied, a rotating priority marker decides.
- From then on, **everyone can see who got which root** — roots stay public all match.
- Picked roots are gone for good. The last round skips picking — leftover roots are just dealt out.

**2. Collect suffixes (one turn per player: 15 s blocking + 10 s picking)**
- Each turn belongs to one player (it rotates). That player first gets **15 private seconds** to **block up to 3 suffixes** — nobody else sees the table until they confirm or time runs out.
- The table then appears to everyone (player count + 10 suffixes on it). Blocked ones are visible but grayed out — nobody can take them this turn.
- In the 10-second pick window you may do **one thing**, in secret: take a suffix, drop one of yours, or pass. Two players can take the **same** suffix — nobody sees who took what.
- Every suffix gets one chance: taken ones never show up on a table again this match, and ones nobody took don't return either. **Blocked suffixes are the exception** — they keep coming back on later tables until someone finally takes them.
- A small board on screen lists every suffix taken so far — each shown once, with no names — so you can guess what people might be holding, but never who holds what.
- You must hold **at least 3 suffixes** before word-building starts; if you're short, the game tops you up automatically over the last three turns.
- Some "suffixes" aren't official ones — they're pieces cut from real words (like *-kaha* out of *kahkaha*). These are bluff fuel.

**3. Build, reveal, accuse (15 s + 15 s)**
- Build one word from **your root + your suffixes** and lock it in. You can't type freely — only combine what you drafted.
- All words are revealed at once. Then everyone gets 15 seconds to secretly **flag one word** they think is invented. You can't flag your own.

## Scoring
- **Real word:** +10 per letter, +30 bonus for the round's longest, +15 per extra suffix chained.
- **Invented word (uydurum):** 0 word points — its payoff is bluffing:
  - **Nobody flags you:** you steal 25 points from every opponent.
  - **Someone flags you:** you pay that same amount, split among everyone who caught you.
  - **You flag a real word by mistake:** you hand half your round score to the person you accused.
- **Leftover suffixes hurt:** every suffix you didn't use costs you 2 × its letters — unless your bluff went unflagged, which waives the penalty. Not submitting means your whole board counts as unused.
- Scores can go negative in a round. Longest valid word wins the round; ties go to whoever used fewer suffixes.

## The strategy triangle
Play it safe with a real word, gamble on a bluff for the steal, or hunt bluffers with your one flag — each option punishes the others, and holding unused suffixes quietly bleeds you either way.

## If someone disconnects
Their seat stays and the game plays it neutrally (they get dealt roots and suffixes, submit nothing). They can rejoin anytime and continue. If fewer than 3 people remain connected, the round finishes and the match ends as it stands. Quitting mid-match forfeits any points that would have come your way.
